###############################################################################
# PHASE 2A
# INDEPENDENT DOCUMENT SIGNATURE VERIFICATION
###############################################################################

This optional technical pass runs only after the exact submitted documents and
their RDE are complete and the RDE is structurally validated, and before the
ImmutableExecutionView is closed. It reads the original document bytes solely
to verify signatures; it does not perform EXTRACTION, add facts to the RDE,
resolve Applicability or execute Requirements/Criteria.

Each verification receipt shall be a separate authenticated, immutable
artifact bound to the exact presentation identity, RDE identity/digest,
source-document identity and byte digest. Preserve verifier identity/version,
provenance, signature mechanism and distinct technical outcomes for byte-range
integrity, cryptographic signature integrity, signer identity, certificate
chain/trust and revocation status. An unavailable check is UNKNOWN, not
positive or negative evidence. A cryptographically intact signature is not
necessarily fully validated.

Receipt authenticity shall be checked against a project-pinned verifier key;
the receipt carries verifier key id, algorithm and signature over its
canonical payload. The admission profile is separately authenticated by a
project-controlled profile-authority key and carries its key id, algorithm
and digest. Pinned key material and its authorization are held outside the
receipt/profile artifact so that an artifact cannot appoint its own signer or
trust anchor. Unknown key, invalid authentication, duplicate/replayed receipt
or altered payload blocks admission.

The verification receipt is not, by itself, admissible Engine input. After the
compiled contract, execution plan and ImmutableExecutionView are frozen, an
authenticated read-only execution binding shall join the receipt to their
identities/digests and to the exact presentation and RDE digest. The binding
does not mutate the receipt. A frozen admission profile shall identify
policy id/version/digest, permitted mechanism, trusted issuer/anchor set and
digest, required cryptographic checks, required signer/issuer role, validation
level and treatment of unknown results. It must be authenticated and applicable
to the active contract. Until a verifier and admission profile satisfying this
contract exist, this interface is preparatory and the Engine returns
MANUAL_REVIEW.

For a DRT, the role check shall establish the source-defined path of either
the RT's digital signature or the issuing council's digital certification.
Recording a signer identity without establishing its role is insufficient.
Trust anchors or certificate policies not available to the local verifier
remain unknown; do not infer public-key trust or current revocation.

No external council or commercial signature API is a required dependency.
Apps Script's native digest functions can establish a digest but do not alone
verify CMS/PAdES signatures, certificate chains or revocation. A local
project-controlled verifier may be used only to the level it can reproduce and
validate against fixtures; unsupported validation levels remain inconclusive.
An image, stamp, QR Code or text description is not cryptographic proof.

If no sufficient authenticated receipt exists, the relevant Criterion may
route to MANUAL_REVIEW under its contract. Digest mismatch, altered receipt,
or presentation/document substitution blocks automatic admission and records
an integrity diagnostic; it cannot create PASS, FAIL or a Nonconformity by
itself. Human verification is a separate authenticated, immutable receipt
binding the exact document/digest and recording reviewer, date, method,
evidence and decision. It never claims cryptographic validation. Human
receipts remain audit context until a criterion-specific authenticated
authority profile has been defined, approved and frozen. This profile is not
currently defined; therefore human-receipt admission remains preparatory and
routes to MANUAL_REVIEW.

The audit-only implementation contract `HumanSignatureReviewCore.js` records
the exact presentation identity, RDE identity/digest, source-document identity/
digest, authenticated active-user identity when Apps Script exposes it, UTC
recording time, review method/details, supporting document identities/digests
and locators, and one of `CONFIRMED_SIGNATURE_PRESENT`, `NOT_CONFIRMED` or
`INCONCLUSIVE`. Its canonical payload is authenticated with a distinct
project-held HMAC-SHA256 key and key id; the secret is never part of the
receipt. Missing key or unavailable active-user identity fails closed. A
successful receipt is tamper-evident and immutable in memory, but this core does
not persist it, establish reviewer authority, establish cryptographic
signature validity, or admit it to the Engine. It always declares
`NOT_EVALUATED_BY_HUMAN_REVIEW` and
`NOT_ESTABLISHED_AUDIT_ONLY`. The Engine has no consumer for this artifact.

END PHASE 2A.

# ENTITY NORMALIZATION AND IMMUTABLE EXECUTION VIEW (PROCESS MEMORY CONTRACT)
###############################################################################

Consume only the completed, immutable RDE.

Normalize representations derived from the RDE using 01_entities.

For every comprovante section represented in the RDE, normalize an exact
OFFICIAL_ESCI_CODE only through the authorized OFFICIAL_ESCI_CODE -> canonical

Do not alter, add, remove or reinterpret any RDE record.

Merge equivalent entities only in the derived normalized representation.

Preserve every reference to the original RDE record and Source Document.

No inference is allowed.

Provide an immutable execution view (the Process Memory contract) exclusively
from the normalized representation derived from the immutable RDE. This view
is a read-only execution contract, not a mandatory materialized or persisted
artifact. When no transformation is needed, a read-only adapter may expose the
validated RDE directly. When normalization is needed, it may exist in memory;
do not persist a second copy of the RDE solely to materialize Process Memory.

The RDE is the only documentary input authorized for the execution view. The
view or read-only adapter shall expose only approved execution interfaces,
canonicalized entities and attributes, TypedReference values when required,
and provenance back to the RDE and source records. Predicates shall not
navigate the raw RDE arbitrarily or bypass the closed Phase 3 view. The view
shall not reopen source documents, invoke extraction, or become a second
documentary source of truth.

Process Memory shall never:

- create or reinterpret documentary facts;
- determine applicability;
- evaluate Requirements;
- evaluate Criteria;
- generate execution results;
- generate Nonconformities.

Close Process Memory as complete and immutable before Phase 4. This closes the
logical execution view, whether backed by an adapter or an in-memory normalized
representation; it does not require persistence.

Requirements shall consume documentary facts from Process Memory only. Criteria
shall consume documentary facts from Process Memory and may consume only the
following authenticated sidecars for their declared purpose: (1) the Phase 3A
sidecar only for a declared DRT-to-product/coverage relation predicate, as
relation-only input, never as an attribute or documentary fact; and (2) the
Phase 2A signature-verification receipt only for a declared SIGNED predicate,
as verification evidence bound to the exact document/presentation, never as
an extracted fact or general attribute. Human-confirmation receipts are audit
context only until an authority profile for the evidence class and Criterion
has been approved; none is defined yet, so they cannot establish PASS. Phase 8
may consume authenticated frozen metadata from either artifact only for
disclosure defined by 06_reports; it shall not recompute correlation or
signature validation, reopen documents or alter consolidated results.
Applicability and all other phases shall consume Process Memory only and shall
not consume these sidecars. This signature sidecar is a narrow approved
exception to the existing Phase 3A relation-only sidecar contract.
ARCHITECTURAL_CONFLICT=RESOLVED_BY_EXPLICIT_USER_DECISION applies to the
relation sidecar only; the signature exception is independently limited to
the declared SIGNED predicate and does not authorize general document access.

Do not reopen Source Documents or retry documentary extraction in this or any
later phase. The single authorized exception is Phase 2A, which reads the
original bytes for signature verification only, before the execution view is
closed; it does not extract documentary facts.

END PHASE 3.

###############################################################################
# PHASE 3A
# ONDA A – ASSISTED DRT/PRODUCT CORRELATION
###############################################################################

This phase may produce a separate, immutable correlation artifact from the
validated RDE and immutable execution view. It shall not modify either source,
create `documentary_associations`, reopen documents, execute Requirements or
Criteria, or produce an EngineResult/PipelineResult.

The artifact shall separate proposals, documentary facts, decisions and
operational gates. Each decision shall be bound to exact RDE references and
digest, exact presentation identity, provenance/integrity, `base_id`,
`binding_type`, frozen policy, threshold, calibration id/version/hash and
validity. The identity follows the authorized `CurrentSubmissionContext`,
using `PROTOCOL_IDENTIFIER` and `REQUEST_DATE` when applicable; without an
identifier, use only the alternative already defined by the current contract.
A decision is not reusable in another presentation, even with identical
documents or RDE digest. The initial
inclusive threshold is `0.80` (`score >= threshold`) and is adjustable only
outside the Engine. The policy and decision are frozen/versioned and the
decision is immutable. Policy and calibration must be valid and applicable to
`base_id` and `binding_type` before the decision; missing, invalid or
inapplicable policy/calibration is `BLOCKED`.

The common correlation contract shall be calibrated independently for each
Knowledge Base and binding type. Auto-admission is available as a capability
but has status `BLOCKED` without adequate empirical calibration. Human review
is required for score below threshold, conflict, ambiguity, competing
candidates, insufficient provenance/integrity, or absent/invalid calibration.
No correlation decision causes PASS in isolation.

Each candidate shall include a versioned conflict assessment with an outcome,
detector identity/version and references to documentary evidence already
included in that candidate. The frozen policy and applicable calibration shall
identify the detector version. Only `NO_MATERIAL_CONFLICT` from that exact
detector version, with evidence covering both endpoints, can pass this gate.
`MATERIAL_CONFLICT`, `INDETERMINATE`, a missing or mismatched assessment, or
evidence that does not cover both endpoints requires human review. An
assessment shall be authenticated over its outcome, calibrated score, detector
id/version, complete candidate evidence references and conflict flags, policy
digest, presentation identity and RDE digest. The
runtime shall reject modified or replayed assessments. The candidate inventory
shall enumerate every DRT-to-eligible-product endpoint pair in the exact RDE;
an omitted, duplicate or unverifiable pair makes the inventory incomplete and
requires human review. An assessment shall use only facts and provenance in the
exact validated RDE; it
shall not infer an explicit documentary association, Requirements, Criteria,
Nonconformities or results. Names or textual resemblance alone do not
establish correspondence or absence of conflict. If the available DRT and
product facts are not comparable under an authorized, calibrated detector, the
assessment is `INDETERMINATE`.

An empirically validated calibration record shall identify both the dataset
digest and the separate validation-evidence digest/reference. A hash alone or a
`VALID` label without that validation record does not open the calibration gate.
The validation receipt shall be authenticated by a calibration-authority key
separate from the policy/configuration signing key; configuration alone cannot
approve its own calibration.
Correlation provenance is sufficient only when the endpoint evidence preserves
usable origin/location: nonempty `sourceKind` and `sourceReference` together, a
positive `page`, `row` or `column`, or a nonempty `text_span`, `location` or
`attachment`. Other nonempty provenance fields alone require human review.

Only an authenticated, read-only interface may expose an admitted correlation
to the Engine. The interface shall bind the decision to the exact presentation
identity, RDE digest, compiled contract, frozen execution plan and immutable
execution view. The authenticated sidecar is limited to contextual documentary
relation for the executor, separate, immutable and read-only; its
authentication covers presentation, RDE, contract, plan and view and it shall
not alter facts, Process Memory or the RDE. The user's decision authorizes this
sidecar as additional authenticated input to the Engine only as a contextual,
relation-only input; Process Memory remains exclusively fact-only. This does
not alter execution semantics or the execution order. A correlation remains
supporting input to a declared contract and cannot create a rule, result,
Requirement, Criterion or Nonconformity.

Phase ordering is explicit. PHASE 3A creates and freezes the correlation
artifact from the validated RDE, its ImmutableExecutionView, the exact current
presentation identity and frozen policy/calibration inputs. It does not require
or read the applicability result or frozen execution plan. After PHASE 4C has
materialized the plan, a separate authenticated read-only Engine binding may
bind the existing artifact to the exact compiled contract, plan and view. This
binding does not mutate or recompute the artifact, reopen documents, revisit
PHASE 3A, or change any completed phase. A blocked or human-review decision
exposes no relation to the Engine.

Any operational presentation of the artifact shall preserve exact RDE text and
RDE references/digest and include the literal disclosure
`Foi utilizada correlação documental automática assistida por inteligência artificial, conforme política e calibração identificadas neste relatório. A validação normativa institucional deste mecanismo permanece pendente. A admissão automática não equivale à confirmação humana da correspondência nem comprova isoladamente o atendimento das exigências normativas.` This phase
does not define or implement the report; report generation remains governed by
Phase 8.

END PHASE 3A.

# PHASE 4A
# SMSCI APPLICABILITY RESOLUTION
###############################################################################

Apply 02a_applicability only after the Process Memory execution view is
complete and immutable.

Select exactly one current comprovante by comparing its PROTOCOL_IDENTIFIER and
canonical civil REQUEST_DATE with the immutable CurrentSubmissionContext
provided by operational intake, as declared in 02a_applicability. A different
request date under the same protocol denotes another presentation. RE_IDENTIFIER
is only a consistency check when available on both sides. processId and
REQUEST_IDENTIFIER do not participate. Do not select by filename, generated or
created timestamp, hash, record_id, attachment count or order. The context is
operational metadata outside the RDE and must be complete, traceable and frozen
before this phase.

Derive productive targets only from canonical entities declared with
APPLICABILITY_TARGET_CLASS OFFICIAL_ESCI_SCOPE in 01_entities. Resolve their
exact official code only through the authorized document, section and mapping
declared in 02a_applicability. Validate that Requirement SMSCI and Criterion
APPLIES_TO references resolve to canonical entities with their declared target
class.

Only after every official decision is complete, derive the internal scopes
declared in 02a_applicability. In particular, a negative SMSCI_IEL decision
from a complete, legible and verifiable official section shall derive only
SMSCI_IN19_APPLICABILITY_REVIEW; it shall not create SMSCI_IEL or determine
whether IN19 applies or is dispensed. An incomplete, illegible or unverifiable
section remains an ARCHITECTURAL_BLOCKER before any derivation.

Evaluate only complete declared applicability rules against authorized,
traceable Process Memory facts.

Alias normalization, textual occurrence, detected entities, document
existence, RPCI orientative text, GLP_MASS_KG and SMSCI metadata on a report,
manual, laudo, DRT or other document required by the target shall never
activate that target.

Every target shall receive an explicit, deterministic and traceable positive
or negative decision before PROCESS.SMSCI is released.

If the selected document is absent or not identified, its section is absent,
incomplete, illegible or unverifiable, its exact code is unknown, a required
fact is absent, a decision conflicts, or traceability is incomplete:

STOP APPLICABILITY RESOLUTION BEFORE THE ENGINE.

Do not emit a partial PROCESS.SMSCI.

Do not create a Criterion result or convert the indeterminacy to
NOT_APPLICABLE, MANUAL_REVIEW, UNKNOWN or FAIL.

END PHASE 4A.

For positive SMSCI_IEL, additionally select IN19 Requirements only when their

For REQ_T1_DRT_SMSCI / T1_DRT_SMSCI_COVERAGE, WORKLIST.SMSCI projects every

No Requirement may be created or rewritten.

END PHASE 4B.

###############################################################################
# PHASE 4C
# PLAN MATERIALIZATION
###############################################################################

INPUT: APPLICABLE_REQUIREMENTS and the release-derived
COMPILED_EXECUTION_INDEX. Freeze APPLICABLE_REQUIREMENTS and consume it only;
do not recalculate, reinterpret or modify Applicability or
IN19_DOCUMENTATION_REGIME.

Materialize the ordered, closed and immutable PLANNED_EXECUTION_UNITS as a
local procedure:

1. For each applicable Requirement in its resolved order, find its block in
   COMPILED_EXECUTION_INDEX and copy every listed UNIT_KEY in that block order.
   UNIT_KEY shall be copied literally. Do not generate, rename, summarize or
   reinterpret it. Semantic labels are not valid substitutes.

2. Preserve the declared TABLE, CONTEXT and every VALIDATE associated with the
   copied Requirement and Criterion. APPLIES_TO shall be consistent with the
   completely resolved PROCESS.SMSCI; any inconsistency stops execution before
   the Engine.

3. When the Requirement block declares ITERATION_SOURCE, materialize one unit
   for each listed UNIT_KEY, resolve and freeze its ITERATION_DOMAIN from that
   ITERATION_SOURCE. Do not materialize a UNIT_KEY per binding.

4. Validate the existing Criterion and Nonconformity references without
   creating or triggering a Nonconformity. Do not execute a Criterion while
   constructing the plan.

5. Freeze PLANNED_EXECUTION_UNITS. Compare its ordered UNIT_KEY sequence for
   exact equality with the ordered sequence selected from
   COMPILED_EXECUTION_INDEX for the applicable Requirements. Any order change,
   absence, extra key or duplicate is EXECUTION_INTEGRITY_ERROR: STOP BEFORE
   EXECUTION.

Each PLANNED_EXECUTION_UNIT contains its literal UNIT_KEY and a reference to
the already resolved Applicability, but no documentary evidence copied from
Process Memory, execution result or anticipated Nonconformity.

ITERATION_DOMAIN defines the complete planned_bindings set. FOR_EACH remains
internal to the Criterion and preserves aggregate result semantics. In
particular, T1_DRT_SMSCI_COVERAGE remains one planned unit with multiple
internal bindings and one aggregate result; it may produce at most one
NC_T1_002 when its declared Criterion result is FAIL.

EACH_REQUIRED_TECHNICAL_RESPONSIBILITY is a context-domain token, not a
documentary fact and not a new UNIT_KEY source. Immediately before execution
of a Criterion that declares this CONTEXT, the execution bridge derives an
ordered resolved responsibility domain only from explicit
REQUIRED_TECHNICAL_RESPONSIBILITY -> CATALOG_IDENTIFIER mappings present in
APPLICABLE_REQUIREMENTS. A generic EACH_REQUIRED_TECHNICAL_RESPONSIBILITY
declaration never creates a responsibility by itself. A Requirement whose
FOR_EACH domain is empty contributes no mapped responsibility.

A mapping identified by a canonical source as UNRESOLVED_ATTRIBUTE is not a
member of this resolved domain and shall not be represented in
02_requirements.txt as a resolved Requirement-to-RT mapping. An unresolved
mapping shall not be converted into TRUE, FALSE, UNKNOWN, NOT_APPLICABLE,
MANUAL_REVIEW, PASS, FAIL or any Nonconformity.

For a resolved responsibility, the bridge may materialize a transient,
immutable ResponsibilityEvidenceBinding authenticated to the exact compiled
contract, frozen execution plan and ImmutableExecutionView.
 The bridge shall consume
documentary records through an intrinsic view snapshot bound to the private
ImmutableExecutionView state; mutable or replaced prototype methods are not an
authorized evidence source. The intrinsic snapshot must capture every helper and
runtime intrinsic it depends on at module initialization, so later reassignment
of global helper bindings cannot alter which private records or facts are
returned. The backing Process Memory store itself must remain
closure-private: no top-level/global lexical binding, exported object, debug
hook or public mutator may expose the WeakMap or permit replacing state for an
existing ImmutableExecutionView. The complete materialization path that writes
records into this private store — including reference-key calculation,
TypedReference validation/copy, JSON-object classification, immutable fact and
provenance copy, strict provenance validation and any recursion used by those
operations — must execute through closure-private helpers captured with the
ImmutableExecutionView implementation. Reassigning same-named global helpers
before projectRdeToExecutionView_ runs shall neither alter a source fact nor
produce a different private Process Memory fact. The binding may
contain zero or more concrete DRT references and their documentary provenance.
It shall not modify the RDE or the plan, and shall not select evidence by
filename, order, proximity, similarity or plausibility. The same concrete DRT
may participate in more than one responsibility only when the declared
catalog/contract permits that reuse.

When execution support for an EACH_REQUIRED_TECHNICAL_RESPONSIBILITY Criterion
is later enabled, evaluations over the resolved responsibility domain remain
internal to the single planned Criterion unit. Aggregate semantics are
universal: FALSE takes precedence over MANUAL_REVIEW, which takes precedence
over UNKNOWN; TRUE is produced only when every evaluated responsibility is
TRUE. NOT_APPLICABLE shall not be used to encode an unresolved mapping.

No list parallel to PLANNED_EXECUTION_UNITS shall be introduced merely to
duplicate its expected, selected or executed Criteria.

END PHASE 4C.

# PHASE 6
# RESULT CONSOLIDATION
###############################################################################

Before consolidation or projection, verify the Plan-to-Results invariant:

- every PLANNED_EXECUTION_UNITS UNIT_KEY produced exactly one result record,
  evaluated or propagated;
- no UNIT_KEY produced two or more results;
- no result exists without a UNIT_KEY in PLANNED_EXECUTION_UNITS;
- for every EVALUATED iterative Criterion, evaluated_bindings equals
  planned_bindings; PROPAGATED records have no evaluated bindings.

If any of these checks fails, stop execution with EXECUTION_INTEGRITY_ERROR.

EXECUTION_INTEGRITY_ERROR is an internal execution error only. It is not a
normative result and shall never be treated as:

PASS, FAIL, NOT_APPLICABLE, MANUAL_REVIEW, UNKNOWN or Nonconformity.

On EXECUTION_INTEGRITY_ERROR:

- stop before IRV/e-SCI projection;
- do not generate a pendency;
- do not alter normative results;
- do not convert the error to MANUAL_REVIEW;
- expose it only through the existing audit/debug channel when supported by the
  architecture;
- do not trigger Report Generation or IRV/e-SCI projection.

Consolidate all Criterion results, preserving PipelineResult for every record
and preserving EngineResult and normalization on EVALUATED records. Propagated
records retain their causal source link instead of receiving an EngineResult
or normalization of their own.

This contract defines results per Criterion and does not define an aggregate
Requirement result. Do not derive or emit a Requirement-level result by
combining Criteria unless a separate canonical contract is approved.

Associate Nonconformities only with FAIL results.

Criterion results shall remain immutable after consolidation.

Associate operational alerts only with MANUAL_REVIEW results.

Operational Alerts are internal execution artifacts.

Before Report Generation,

every Operational Alert shall be translated into exactly one
MANUAL_REVIEW report entry.

Operational Alert identifiers shall never appear in user-visible reports.

MANUAL_REVIEW results shall never generate Nonconformities.

MANUAL_REVIEW shall propagate to every dependent Criterion unless an explicit documentary rule defines otherwise.

Propagation is represented explicitly. Every planned UNIT_KEY produces exactly
one result record with `result_origin` equal to `EVALUATED` or `PROPAGATED`.

An EVALUATED record represents a Criterion actually executed and shall contain
`evaluation_performed=true`, `engine_result`, `pipeline_result` and
`normalization`, plus the predicate trace and evidence references required by
the Criterion contract.

A PROPAGATED record represents a dependent Criterion that was not executed and
shall contain `evaluation_performed=false`, `source_unit` or
`source_criterion`, `propagation_cause` and `pipeline_result=MANUAL_REVIEW`.
It shall not fabricate an EngineResult for the dependent Criterion, predicate
bindings, predicate trace or evidence. The source link shall preserve the
causal chain, including `UNKNOWN -> UNKNOWN_TO_MANUAL_REVIEW -> MANUAL_REVIEW`
when that is the originating result. Propagation never produces FAIL,
Nonconformity, facts or altered evidence.

Do not modify previous results.

No report section shall be generated before Result Consolidation completes.

# PHASE 6A
# IRV OPERATIONAL PROJECTION
###############################################################################

Only after Result Consolidation completes, consume the immutable consolidated
Criterion results and their declared Nonconformity associations.

For each consolidated FAIL associated with a Nonconformity that declares
IRV_TABLE, IRV_CRITERION and IRV_CAUSE in 05_nonconformities, create exactly
one transient operational pending-entry. Load IRV_SUBCAUSE only when declared;
the selectable leaf is IRV_SUBCAUSE when present, otherwise IRV_CAUSE. Preserve
IRV_CAUSE as the internal parent when a subcause is present. Preserve consolidated result order and the complete traceability of the source
FAIL and documentary evidence.

The operational projection is not a Criterion result, a Nonconformity or
documentary evidence. It shall never:

- create, remove, merge or modify a FAIL;
- create or modify a Nonconformity;
- execute a Requirement or Criterion;
- recalculate Applicability or PROCESS.SMSCI;
- reopen a Source Document or the RDE;
- infer an IRV item, cause or subcause by similarity;
- create an artificial IRV_SUBCAUSE.

Do not group, deduplicate or suppress pending entries because they share the
same IRV_CRITERION. Distinct selectable leaves — IRV_SUBCAUSE when declared,
otherwise IRV_CAUSE — shall remain distinct operational pending entries.

FOR_EACH is a structural aggregation inside one Criterion evaluation. Its
instances shall produce one consolidated Criterion result and, on FAIL, one
association with the declared Nonconformity. Repeated failing instances with
the same Nonconformity and the same selectable IRV leaf are evidence of that
single pending entry, not additional pending entries. Preserve and aggregate
their traceable evidence for a minimal DESCRIPTION when needed. This rule shall
never merge distinct Nonconformities or distinct selectable IRV leaves.

When a triggered Nonconformity has no declared IRV projection, preserve its
FAIL and route the missing operational projection to the report's human
treatment section. Do not invent an e-SCI item or cause and do not convert the
FAIL to MANUAL_REVIEW.

For DESCRIPTION_POLICY MINIMAL_IF_NEEDED, attach a DESCRIPTION only when a
concrete fact already present in the consolidated result and its evidence is
needed to identify the divergence. Simple documentary absence requires no
additional description. DESCRIPTION shall normally contain one sentence of no
more than 180 characters; 180 is a preference, not a validity limit. The
absolute limit is 500 characters. DESCRIPTION shall not reproduce the complete
IRV_CAUSE, IRV_SUBCAUSE or IRV_CRITERION. If no description is needed,
project exactly "Não necessário." for report rendering.

Derive candidate technical counters only from the canonical consolidated
result set before rendering. Validate:

VERIFICATIONS_EXECUTED counts only result records with
result_origin=EVALUATED:
PASS + FAIL + NOT_APPLICABLE + MANUAL_REVIEW.
PROPAGATED records are counted separately as VERIFICATIONS_PROPAGATED and do
not enter VERIFICATIONS_EXECUTED.

If the equality is not satisfied, record REPORT_COUNTER_INCONSISTENCY as an
audit-only diagnostic and omit the invalid technical totals. Never fabricate,
adjust or reconstruct a counter from rendered text blocks. The inconsistency
shall not create or modify PASS, FAIL, NOT_APPLICABLE, MANUAL_REVIEW,
Nonconformities or operational pending entries. Continue operational report
generation when the canonical results and operational projection remain
intact; expose the inconsistency explicitly only in audit mode.

The transient operational projection becomes immutable before Phase 8.

END PHASE 6A.

alter the decision; it shall only disclose these frozen projections and shall
