DRT_PRODUCT_CORRELATION_IS_EXTERNAL_TO_ENGINE

The Engine shall not generate, score, calibrate, admit or mutate an assisted
DRT/product correlation. Correlation proposals, documentary facts, decisions,
policy versions, calibration versions, provenance and integrity metadata
belong to a separate immutable artifact.

The Engine shall consume that artifact only through an authenticated,
read-only interface bound to the exact compiled contract, frozen execution
plan, ImmutableExecutionView, RDE digest and exact presentation identity. The
interface shall reject reuse in another presentation, even when documents and
RDE digest are identical, and shall reject absent, invalid,
conflicting, ambiguous, competing, below-threshold or insufficiently
provenanced/integrity-checked decisions. The initial inclusive threshold is
0.80 and is configured and versioned outside the Engine. The policy,
threshold, calibration id/version/hash and validity shall be frozen before the
decision. Policy and calibration shall be valid and applicable to the active
Knowledge Base and binding type; otherwise the decision is BLOCKED.

Auto-admission is supported as a capability but is BLOCKED unless empirical
calibration is adequate, valid and applicable to the active Knowledge Base
and binding type. A correlation decision shall never, by itself, produce
PASS, satisfy a Criterion, alter a Requirement, create a Nonconformity or
reclassify documentary evidence as documentary_association.

RESPONSIBILITY_HAS_TECHNICAL_PRODUCT

This relation-only predicate is declared for the current authorized tuple
RT_002_EXECUCAO_DE_OBRA and CONFORMITY_REPORT only. It returns TRUE only when
the exact RT-002 DRT binding is present, the exact CONFORMITY_REPORT endpoint
has entity type REPORT, and the relation is supplied by an explicit
documentary association read from the canonical ImmutableExecutionView derived
from the parser-validated RDE. An explicit documentary-association route may
be consumed only when exactly one validated RDE association binds that DRT to
that exact report endpoint and carries its source-document reference and
literal statement text; available provenance is preserved. This RDE-origin
check is the trust boundary for an explicit documentary fact; per-association
provenance remains optional under the RDE contract. The association is
contextual evidence only; it remains FACT-ONLY and is not rewritten or
promoted into another association type. An ambiguous explicit association
requires MANUAL_REVIEW. Invalid RDE associations are rejected at validation
before execution. If an independently authenticated admitted correlation
sidecar is the only relation available, it is traceable contextual input and
the predicate returns MANUAL_REVIEW; the inferred relation alone shall never
produce TRUE, PASS, satisfy the Criterion or create a Nonconformity.
Because this tuple is REQUIRED, FALSE is permitted only when the exact
CONFORMITY_REPORT endpoint is absent from the validated execution view.
Missing or ambiguous RT-002 evidence, or a missing or blocked relation, is
MANUAL_REVIEW and never establishes product absence. The relation is
contextual input only; it cannot create facts or evidence, alter Process
Memory or documentary_associations, produce another Requirement/Criterion
result, or create a Nonconformity.

This predicate consumes only canonical DRT evidence references and derives all

not supply an arbitrary scope-item collection. The resolver authenticates the

# COUNCIL STATUS CLASSIFICATION VOCABULARY

The source statuses in ART (CREA), RRT (CAU) and TRT (CFT) remain independent
documentary facts. Consumers may use only these controlled semantic categories:

- registration: `REGISTERED`, `NOT_REGISTERED`, `DRAFT`, `UNKNOWN`, `OTHER`;
- issuance: `ISSUED`, `NOT_ISSUED`, `DRAFT`, `UNKNOWN`, `OTHER`;
- payment: `PAID`, `UNPAID`, `UNKNOWN`, `OTHER`.

Each classification must preserve the exact source literal and provenance and
must come from an explicit, versioned mapping keyed by document type, issuing
council and status field. There is no global lexical equivalence among ART,
RRT and TRT and no substring, synonym or model-based normalization. Missing or
ambiguous facts and unmapped literals are `UNKNOWN`/`OTHER` and require
`MANUAL_REVIEW`; they do not by themselves produce `FAIL` or a Nonconformity.
For the `REGISTERED` Criteria, the IRV condition is conjunctive: registration
is `REGISTERED`, issuance is `ISSUED`, payment is `PAID`, and no status says
`DRAFT`. Until explicit mappings for each council/document type are supplied,
this vocabulary is preparatory and does not authorize the Engine to classify
raw status strings.

COUNCIL_STATE, COUNCIL_REGISTRATION_STATUS, COUNCIL_ISSUANCE_STATUS AND
COUNCIL_PAYMENT_STATUS ARE DOCUMENTARY FACTS. THEY SHALL NOT, BY THEMSELVES,
CREATE A CONCLUSION ABOUT PROFESSIONAL REGULARITY, PROFESSIONAL HABILITATION OR
COMPLIANCE.

RESPONSIBILITY_TYPE IS A DOCUMENTARY FACT ABOUT THE NATURE EXPLICITLY
IDENTIFIED IN THE DRT. EXECUTION MAY USE IT ONLY THROUGH A DECLARED
RESPONSIBILITY-EVIDENCE BINDING CONTRACT. ABSENCE, AMBIGUITY OR A DIFFERENT
DOCUMENTARY VALUE SHALL NOT BE NORMALIZED, GUESSED OR RECLASSIFIED.

RESPONSIBILITY_EVIDENCE_MATCHES_PROCESS_RI_LEGAL_ENTITY RETURNS FALSE ONLY
WHEN DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE IDENTIFIES THE RESPONSIBLE PARTY IN THE EVIDENCE
DOCUMENT AND THE RESPONSIBLE PARTY IN THE PROCESS AS DISTINCT LEGAL PERSONS.

FOR LEGAL PERSONS, A DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE CNPJ IN THE PROCESS AND THE CNPJ
IN THE EVIDENCE DOCUMENT, BY ITSELF, DOES NOT IDENTIFY DISTINCT LEGAL PERSONS
AND SHALL NOT PRODUCE A FAIL. THIS INCLUDES CNPJS OF THE HEAD OFFICE AND OF A
BRANCH OF THE SAME LEGAL PERSON.

RESPONSIBILITY_EVIDENCE_MATCHES_PROCESS_RI_LEGAL_ENTITY RETURNS TRUE WHEN THE
DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE IDENTIFIES THE SAME LEGAL PERSON, INCLUDING A HEAD OFFICE
AND ITS BRANCH, OR WHEN THE CNPJ DIFFERENCE IS THE ONLY AVAILABLE EVIDENCE.

RESPONSIBILITY_EVIDENCE_MATCHES_PROCESS_BASIC_DATA IS THE VALIDATION GATE FOR
REQ_T1_DRT_BASIC_DATA. It consumes only typed facts from the immutable view of
the authenticated current presentation. A comparison requires one exact
REQUERIMENTO_ESCI source, sufficient provenance, and an explicit documentary
association between that request and each compared DRT. RT comparison also
requires one uniquely resolved required responsibility and its canonical DRT
evidence binding. The RT comparand is TECHNICAL_RESPONSIBLE_NAME_TEXT; it is
never REQUESTER_NAME_TEXT. Address comparison uses the request's separately
extracted address components and the DRT's literal address, accepting only an
exact ordered token match after punctuation/whitespace separation; no name,
abbreviation, geocoding or semantic normalization is performed. Area comparison
uses PROPERTY_TOTAL_CONSTRUCTED_AREA_TEXT only, and a DRT value is comparable
only when it explicitly states "área total construída no terreno" with an
exact numeric literal and m² unit. It never substitutes land area, block area,
or another scope. The predicate returns TRUE only for a complete exact match.
Missing, ambiguous, unlinked, differently formatted or differing values return
MANUAL_REVIEW; this validation gate never returns FALSE or creates a
Nonconformity. RI remains MANUAL_REVIEW unless a reliable document explicitly
identifies the RI and is bound to the same DRT. A text difference alone is not
proof of a normative violation.

# EXECUTION MODEL
###############################################################################

The results registered below are internal EngineResult values. They are not
the external PipelineResult. The boundary mapping, including the traceable
normalization of UNKNOWN, is defined by 08_execution_pipeline.txt.

FOR EACH CRITERION

SET ASSERT_RESULT = TRUE

    IF APPLIES_TO IS NOT PRESENT IN PROCESS.SMSCI

        REGISTER RESULT NOT_APPLICABLE
        REGISTER CRITERION
        REGISTER REQUIREMENT
        CONTINUE

    END

    FOR EACH VALIDATE DECLARED IN THE ACTIVE REQUIREMENT

        EXECUTE THE CORRESPONDING VALIDATION FUNCTION

        IF ANY VALIDATION RETURNS FALSE

            SET ASSERT_RESULT = FALSE

        ELSE IF ANY VALIDATION RETURNS MANUAL_REVIEW

            SET ASSERT_RESULT = MANUAL_REVIEW

        ELSE IF ANY VALIDATION RETURNS UNKNOWN
            AND ASSERT_RESULT IS NOT FALSE

            SET ASSERT_RESULT = UNKNOWN

        END

    END

    IF ASSERT_RESULT IS NOT FALSE
       AND ASSERT_RESULT IS NOT MANUAL_REVIEW

        EXECUTE ASSERT
        
        IF ASSERT RETURNS FALSE

    SET ASSERT_RESULT = FALSE

ELSE IF ASSERT RETURNS MANUAL_REVIEW
    AND ASSERT_RESULT IS NOT FALSE

    SET ASSERT_RESULT = MANUAL_REVIEW

ELSE IF ASSERT RETURNS UNKNOWN
    AND ASSERT_RESULT IS TRUE

    SET ASSERT_RESULT = UNKNOWN

ELSE IF ASSERT RETURNS NOT_APPLICABLE
    AND ASSERT_RESULT IS TRUE
    AND ASSERT RESULT_CONTRACT DECLARES NOT_APPLICABLE

    SET ASSERT_RESULT = NOT_APPLICABLE

ELSE IF ASSERT RETURNS NOT_APPLICABLE

    RAISE EXECUTION_INTEGRITY_ERROR

END

    END

    IF ASSERT_RESULT = TRUE

        REGISTER RESULT TRUE
        REGISTER CRITERION
        REGISTER REQUIREMENT
        REGISTER DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE

    ELSE IF ASSERT_RESULT = FALSE

        REGISTER RESULT FALSE
        REGISTER CRITERION
        REGISTER REQUIREMENT
        REGISTER DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE
        REGISTER NONCONFORMITY FROM FAIL

    ELSE IF ASSERT_RESULT = MANUAL_REVIEW

        REGISTER RESULT MANUAL_REVIEW
        REGISTER CRITERION
        REGISTER REQUIREMENT
        REGISTER DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE
        REGISTER OPERATIONAL_ALERT FROM MANUAL_REVIEW

    ELSE IF ASSERT_RESULT = NOT_APPLICABLE

        REGISTER RESULT NOT_APPLICABLE
        REGISTER CRITERION
        REGISTER REQUIREMENT
        REGISTER DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE WHEN AVAILABLE

    ELSE

        REGISTER RESULT UNKNOWN
        REGISTER CRITERION
        REGISTER REQUIREMENT
        REGISTER DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE

    END

END

DOCUMENT_ABSENCE IS VALID_EVIDENCE

DOCUMENT_ABSENCE IS VALID EVIDENCE ONLY WHEN AN AUTHENTICATED, COMPLETE
DOCUMENT INVENTORY IS BOUND TO THE EXACT PRESENTATION AND RDE AND ESTABLISHES
THAT THE RELEVANT DOCUMENT WAS NOT SUBMITTED. AN EMPTY ENTITY COLLECTION, A
MISSING DOCUMENTARY ASSOCIATION, OR AN UNRESOLVED LOOKUP DOES NOT ESTABLISH
DOCUMENT_ABSENCE. THE CURRENT CONTRACT DEFINES NO SUCH COMPLETE-INVENTORY FACT;
PHASE 2 DECLARES DOCUMENT STATES BUT DOES NOT DEFINE THEIR REPRESENTATION,
COMPLETENESS TEST OR AUTHENTICATED BINDING TO THE EXACT PRESENTATION AND RDE.
UNTIL THAT CONTRACT IS RECONCILED, EXECUTION SHALL NOT SYNTHESIZE
DOCUMENT_ABSENCE OR FALSE FROM THESE CONDITIONS. WHEN NO EVIDENCE CAN BE
BOUND AND COMPLETENESS IS NOT ESTABLISHED, THE AFFECTED CRITERION'S EVIDENCE
INPUT REMAINS UNKNOWN AND IS EVALUATED ONLY UNDER THAT CRITERION'S DECLARED
LOGIC. PRESERVE CRITERION-LEVEL RESULTS AND DECLARED COMPOSITION PRECEDENCE;
THE PIPELINE MAY NORMALIZE AN UNKNOWN CRITERION RESULT TO MANUAL_REVIEW. KEEP
TRACEABLE SOURCE CONTEXT AND DO NOT CREATE A NONCONFORMITY FROM THIS CONDITION.
